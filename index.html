The Faces of Struggle.
Guts is the Black Swordsman, a warrior who once fought alongside the Band of the Hawk. After betrayal and tragedy tear his world apart, he travels with the Dragonslayer, battling apostles and struggling against the fate that follows him.
Casca was once a proud commander of the Band of the Hawk, fighting alongside Griffith and Guts. Her journey takes her from a devoted soldier to a woman forced to endure unimaginable loss, betrayal, and struggle.
Griffith was the charismatic leader of the Band of the Hawk, admired by many for his vision and determination. However, his journey is marked by a tragic fall from grace, leading to devastating consequences for those around him.
Skull Knight is a mysterious warrior who has wandered the world for centuries. He stands against the God Hand and often appears when darkness is near. His true identity and past remain shrouded in mystery, making him one of the most intriguing figures in Berserk.
“Take heed struggler, you were born from a corpse and began your life from death in the mud! You are closer to death than anyone. Thus you excel in escaping it.”

— The Skull Knight
